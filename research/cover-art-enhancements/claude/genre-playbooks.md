# Genre Playbooks

One entry per book category. Find the matching `category` id from the cover brief, then use the entry as the *starting constraints* for concept, archetype, palette and lettering. Variants of the same book must still differ from each other (see the skill), but all stay inside the playbook's guard rails.

Entry format: **Promise** (what the cover must signal) · **Archetypes** (ranked) · **Palette** · **Lettering** · **Imagery & medium** · **Avoid**.

---

## Audience dial (applies to every category)

| Audience | Saturation | Complexity | Lettering | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ages 3–6 | High chroma, clean | Very low; one hero | Chunky, rounded, large | Warm, safe, joyful |
| Ages 6–9 | Vivid with shading | Low–medium | Bold, friendly, hand-lettered | Playful, curious |
| Ages 9–12 | Bold, higher contrast | Medium; dynamic angle | Bold geometric / slab, outline | Adventurous |
| Teens | Limited / duotone, graphic | Medium; poster-like | Stylized, expressive | Cool, confident |
| Adults | Muted base + one accent | Medium–high, controlled | Refined, editorial | Sophisticated |

---

## `kids-reading` — early readers and reading books (ages 3–7)
- **Promise:** warm, safe, "I can read this one."
- **Archetypes:** Iconic Hero · Scene Window · Frame & Emblem
- **Palette:** clean high-chroma primaries on warm cream or soft sky; 3–4 hues.
- **Lettering:** chunky rounded hand-lettered capitals, very large, high contrast, 2–4 stacked lines.
- **Imagery & medium:** gouache or flat-vector picture-book rendering; one expressive character with a clear emotion plus one prop that connects to the title.
- **Avoid:** scary lighting, clutter, tiny text, photoreal rendering.

## `kids-educational` — workbooks and learning books (ages 3–10)
- **Promise:** playful but organized; "I will learn something and enjoy it."
- **Archetypes:** Pattern Field · Paper-Cut Collage · Iconic Hero
- **Palette:** bright, orderly, 4 hues max; school-supply cues (pencil yellow, chalkboard green, notebook blue).
- **Lettering:** bold friendly sans/rounded capitals; optional age badge (exact text from the brief only).
- **Imagery & medium:** subject-linked motifs (letters-as-shapes *without legible characters*, pencils, planets, animals) arranged on a tidy grid or in a collage.
- **Avoid:** legible letters/numbers in the art, worksheet-looking clutter, dull corporate blue.

## `kids-storybook` — picture and story books
- **Promise:** an emotional story moment; "I want to know what happens."
- **Archetypes:** Scene Window · Layered Depth · Frame & Emblem
- **Palette:** painterly and atmospheric; one time of day (golden hour, moonlit, misty morning) drives the palette.
- **Lettering:** warm hand-lettered title with a tiny flourish; sits in the sky or ground quiet zone.
- **Imagery & medium:** watercolor-and-gouache or soft digital painting; character mid-action or mid-discovery; story props hint at the plot.
- **Avoid:** static front-facing mascot poses, flat even lighting, generic forest backdrop.

## `kids-nonfiction` — informative books (ages 7–12)
- **Promise:** "Amazing facts inside": awe and clarity.
- **Archetypes:** Layered Depth · Iconic Hero · Split / Diagonal
- **Palette:** saturated and bold; dark luminous grounds work for space/ocean/dinosaurs; clean bright grounds for animals/science.
- **Lettering:** bold geometric or slab capitals with outline; strong contrast.
- **Imagery & medium:** subject hero at a dynamic angle (low angle, cutaway, scale comparison); semi-realistic illustration.
- **Avoid:** infographic labels (no legible text beyond the declared layers), cartoonish tone for older kids, cluttered fact collage.

## `teen-nonfiction` — teen and young-adult informative or stylish titles
- **Promise:** graphic, confident, "not a kids' book."
- **Archetypes:** Type-as-Image · Split / Diagonal · Minimal Poster
- **Palette:** duotone or limited palette with one hot accent; high contrast.
- **Lettering:** oversized, expressive, asymmetrical placement; tight leading.
- **Imagery & medium:** poster-like graphic illustration or stylized photo-collage look; mood over literalism.
- **Avoid:** childish mascots, rainbow palettes, decorative frames.

## `adult-nonfiction` — informative and general adult nonfiction
- **Promise:** credible, considered, editorial.
- **Archetypes:** Minimal Poster · Type-as-Image · Gallery Plate
- **Palette:** muted base (warm paper, slate, deep green, ink) plus one deliberate accent.
- **Lettering:** refined serif or clean grotesque; generous tracking; hierarchy with calm scale contrast.
- **Imagery & medium:** a single strong visual metaphor; generous negative space; subtle texture (paper, ink, cloth).
- **Avoid:** stock-photo clichés, busy collage, glossy gradients, clip-art.

## `coloring-kids` — kids' coloring books
- **Promise:** fun, clean, "I can color this right now."
- **Archetypes:** Iconic Hero · Pattern Field · Paper-Cut Collage
- **Palette:** cheerful pastel or vivid grounds with confetti shapes; colored samples in marker/crayon feel.
- **Lettering:** playful, chunky, rounded, with crayon-like outline.
- **Imagery & medium:** hero character drawn with **thick, clean outlines**, partly colored in, rest left as pure line art to show the interior style honestly.
- **Avoid:** fine intricate detail (misrepresents the interior), dark moody grounds, photo-real shading.

## `coloring-adult` — adult coloring books
- **Promise:** calm, intricate, beautiful; "a creative escape."
- **Archetypes:** Frame & Emblem · Gallery Plate · Pattern Field
- **Palette:** jewel tones glowing on deep ink/indigo/charcoal, *or* refined cream ground with soft pencil colors and a metallic accent.
- **Lettering:** elegant serif capitals, widely spaced, or serif + delicate script accent; foil/emboss feel permitted.
- **Imagery & medium:** intricate fine-line art, partially colored with colored pencil or gel pen texture; ornamental frame or botanical border.
- **Avoid:** childish fonts, thick cartoon outlines, neon.

## `mandala` — mandala coloring books
- **Promise:** meditative, symmetrical, precise.
- **Archetypes:** Frame & Emblem · Pattern Field · Minimal Poster
- **Palette:** jewel tones on near-black or deep indigo; or cream with gold and one muted accent.
- **Lettering:** spaced elegant capitals, set in a quiet band or inside the ring; thin gold or white.
- **Imagery & medium:** one large mandala, perfect radial symmetry, partly colored in, outer rings left as line art; geometric and floral motifs.
- **Avoid:** sacred religious iconography, off-center asymmetry in the mandala itself, cluttered corners.

## `puzzle` — word search, maze, sudoku, spot-the-difference, crosswords
- **Promise:** "challenge and fun" with an instantly recognizable puzzle motif.
- **Archetypes:** Pattern Field · Split / Diagonal · Iconic Hero
- **Palette:** high-contrast and clean; kids: bright and playful; adults: classic (navy, cream, one accent).
- **Lettering:** bold, highly legible, slightly geometric.
- **Imagery & medium:** puzzle motif as hero (a maze path forming a shape, an abstract grid, interlocking pieces). **Grids must be abstract, with no legible letters or numbers.**
- **Avoid:** legible puzzle contents, visual noise behind the title, low contrast.

## `sketch-pencil` — pencil and artistic sketch books
- **Promise:** craft, intimacy, hand-made skill.
- **Archetypes:** Gallery Plate · Minimal Poster · Iconic Hero
- **Palette:** warm off-white or toned paper, graphite greys, one accent smear (red chalk, sepia, or a single watercolor wash).
- **Lettering:** thin hand-drawn capitals or typewriter-like, restrained.
- **Imagery & medium:** convincing graphite/charcoal shading, visible construction lines, paper tooth, smudges, torn or taped edge details.
- **Avoid:** digital-clean gradients, heavy color, ornate frames.

## `painting` — oil and watercolor painting books
- **Promise:** the painting *is* the cover; gallery quality.
- **Archetypes:** Gallery Plate · Scene Window · Minimal Poster
- **Palette:** follow the painting: oil = rich, chiaroscuro, earthy depth; watercolor = luminous, soft, paper white breathing through.
- **Lettering:** refined serif in cream, white or muted gold, placed in a calm passage of the painting or on the margin.
- **Imagery & medium:** visible brush texture (impasto, wet-edge bleeds, canvas weave); loose edges; deliberate unpainted areas for watercolor.
- **Avoid:** airbrushed smoothness, photographic sharpness, plastic gloss.

## `anime` — 2D / 3D anime illustration books
- **Promise:** a key-visual poster; style and energy.
- **Archetypes:** Layered Depth · Scene Window · Split / Diagonal
- **Palette:** saturated gradients with a clear complementary accent; strong rim light, bokeh or light particles.
- **Lettering:** stylized brush, neon or sleek display lettering; may overlap the character subtly.
- **Imagery & medium:** 2D: cel-shaded, clean line weight variation, dynamic pose, wind-blown hair/cloth. 3D: soft stylized 3D render with subsurface skin and cinematic lighting. **Original characters only.**
- **Avoid:** recognizable franchise characters or costumes, dead-flat standing poses, muddy skin tones.

## `digital-art` — digital drawings and concept art
- **Promise:** cinematic and imaginative.
- **Archetypes:** Layered Depth · Scene Window · Iconic Hero
- **Palette:** atmospheric with a strong key light; limited palette with one saturated accent.
- **Lettering:** cinematic display type (clean, wide, metallic or etched) in a quiet zone.
- **Imagery & medium:** painterly digital rendering, strong silhouette, atmospheric haze, scale contrast.
- **Avoid:** over-rendered noise, generic fantasy cliché, lens-flare overload.

## `photography` — photography books
- **Promise:** one extraordinary photograph and quiet confidence.
- **Archetypes:** Gallery Plate · Minimal Poster · Scene Window
- **Palette:** natural color from the photograph; golden-hour / blue-hour grades; neutral frame.
- **Lettering:** small, refined, widely spaced; title on negative space or in the frame margin.
- **Imagery & medium:** convincingly photographic (lens, depth of field, light quality, subtle grain), full-bleed or framed with a generous border.
- **Avoid:** CGI or HDR look, text over busy areas, heavy filters.

## `photorealism` — photorealistic subject books
- **Promise:** "how is this real?"; precision and wonder.
- **Archetypes:** Minimal Poster · Iconic Hero · Gallery Plate
- **Palette:** controlled studio or natural light, restrained palette, one accent.
- **Lettering:** minimal, elegant, high contrast on negative space.
- **Imagery & medium:** macro detail, shallow depth of field, tactile surfaces (water droplets, feather barbs, metal grain).
- **Avoid:** uncanny faces, plastic skin, over-sharpening.

## `all-ages` — family and general all-ages books
- **Promise:** warm, inclusive, broad appeal.
- **Archetypes:** Iconic Hero · Scene Window · Paper-Cut Collage
- **Palette:** moderate saturation, warm neutrals with two lively accents; no gender-coded defaults.
- **Lettering:** friendly but not babyish; mid-weight, hand-lettered or soft geometric.
- **Imagery & medium:** charming stylized illustration with a clear story hint.
- **Avoid:** age-specific tropes, harsh contrast, cynical tone.
