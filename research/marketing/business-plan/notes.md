# Demand

Print activity books (coloring, puzzles) is a growing market. Etsy digital downloads carry ~85–90% gross margins, but same is true for every competitor. The durable advantage is niche selection, quality control, compliance, brand and a data feedback loop, not generation speed.

Kids' coloring and activity books, themed and large-print puzzle books, and seasonal printable kits score highest.
