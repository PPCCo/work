# Interests

Translating broad consumer hobbies into highly monetisable publishing niches for activity, coloring, and puzzle books is all about identifying targeted passions. Highly generalized themes (like "Generic Mandalas" or "Basic Word Searches") are incredibly oversaturated, whereas specific sub-hobbies command premium pricing and repeat buyers. 💡 2026 Design Trends for Book Publishing

If you are planning to self-publish (e.g., via Amazon KDP) or manufacture these books, implement these two major consumer preferences:
1. Grayscale Photo Coloring vs. Line Art: Standard line art is declining, while grayscale shading (where users color over a black-and-white photo) is booming because it makes the final product look highly professional. [1] (https://www.kdpeasy.com/guides/coloring-book-trends-2026)
2. Finished Sample Covers: Covers showing a partially colored-in or completely finished artistic sample on a crisp white background drastically outperform plain line-art covers in online click-through rates. [1] (https://www.kdpeasy.com/guides/coloring-book-trends-2026)
