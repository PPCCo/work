# Context

My current `.claude/` framework is designed to autonomously create coloring books, puzzle books, etc. This framework contains AI skills, agents, workflows, standards, scripts, rules, etc that helps develop these books in an automated and autonomous fashion.

Using this same `.claude` framework, in addition to creating coloring books, puzzle books, etc for different age groups, I also want to autonomously generate digital and printable products for selling on Etsy.

---

In addition to the product types I specified, as an alternate to Coloring Books, I also want to have an alternate version, a digital download of individual coloring pages as (digital downloadable pdf).
