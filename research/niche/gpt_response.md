# 50 different digital product on Etsy that are "niche" products with "high" demand and "low" competition

---

The most promising Etsy digital-product opportunities are not necessarily the broad categories with the highest search interest. They are often specific, practical products for a clearly defined buyer, where the product solves a recurring problem better than a generic printable.

For this research, I used Etsy's latest available Fall/Winter 2026 Seller Trend Report, published on 14 October 2026, along with current public listings and Etsy's own seller guidance. Etsy reported that searches for DIY activities rose by 8,500%, personalised friend gifts by 1,400%, and pet-gifting searches doubled; Friendsmas party-supply sales rose by 37%. These are useful market signals, but they do not prove demand for any particular digital download.&#x20;

I also found that seemingly attractive markets such as Airbnb welcome books, general home-maintenance binders, teacher planners and generic pet-sitter forms already have visible competitors. The opportunity is to differentiate through a narrower audience, a more useful workflow, better functionality or a specific buying occasion—not simply to create another large bundle.&#x20;

One important limitation: actual Etsy search-volume and listing-competition measurements require its Marketplace Insights tool. I could not access account-level keyword metrics in this research session, so the shortlist distinguishes observed market evidence from estimated white-space opportunities rather than pretending that all 50 products have proven low competition. Etsy itself recommends using Marketplace Insights to compare search activity and listing availability.&#x20;

## How I recommend using the research

First, select six concepts from the workbook and validate the two suggested keyword phrases for each using Etsy Marketplace Insights. Record search activity, matching listing counts and the strengths and weaknesses of the first 20–30 competing results.

Next, build just three minimum viable products. Use AI agents for research, structuring content, generating original draft material, creating spreadsheet formulas, producing listing mockups and checking quality. Keep human review for accuracy, usability, intellectual-property rights and safety-sensitive content.

One policy detail matters: Etsy requires sellers to disclose seller-prompted AI creations in the listing description, and its Creativity Standards exclude AI prompt bundles from qualifying as designed-by-seller digital products.&#x20;
