## How to make the listings more likely to attract buyers

For each product, create seven to ten listing images that answer the buyer's questions:

1. Cover image: One clear product promise, readable title and a realistic preview.
2. What's included: Show every major file or component.
3. Inside the product: Display actual pages, game cards or templates.
4. How it works: Three simple steps from purchase to use.
5. Who it's for: Show the intended family, host, pet owner or recipient.
6. Personalisation or variations: Demonstrate different styles or options where available.
7. File details: State page sizes, file formats, editing requirements and printing instructions.
8. Digital-download notice: Clearly state that no physical item will be shipped.

Use AI for original illustrations, layouts, first drafts, and product variations, but personally test every game, check every worksheet, and proofread all text. Etsy permits seller-prompted AI creations when they meet its Creativity Standards, with AI use disclosed in the listing description.
