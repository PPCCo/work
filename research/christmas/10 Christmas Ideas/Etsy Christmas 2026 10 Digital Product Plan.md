# Etsy Christmas 2026: 10 Digital Product Plan

Oct 21, 2026 · @Q

## How to use this plan

This plan specifies 10 Christmas digital products for Etsy that an AI tool can build end to end: files, mockups and listing copy. Work in tier and "Live by" order, and treat every number as direction, not a forecast.

**Instructions for the AI builder**

- For each product: build the files, run its QA gates, make the 10 listing images, write the listing from the Global standards section, then return a publish checklist. Do not publish without the owner's approval.
- `[SHOP]` is the shop name. Ask once for the shop name, currency and logo. Never invent reviews, sales figures or testimonials.
- Etsy's listing editor overrides this doc on limits, categories and attributes, which change. If they differ, follow the editor and flag it.
- Correctness beats polish. Spreadsheets, pairing logic and puzzles must be tested by code before listing.

**Ideas considered and dropped**

- Generic Christmas wall art, clipart and SVG bundles. Saturated, and Etsy's Mark of the Maker theme says buyers want visible human work. AI-only art is also the category most exposed to enforcement.
- Cross-stitch and other pattern PDFs. Near-identical listings dominate the sample, and chart quality is hard to verify automatically.
- AI prompt bundles and resold PLR or templates. Etsy's [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity) exclude AI prompt bundles and PDFs of someone else's work.
- Canva or Corjl link templates. They add a manual step and a third-party dependency. This plan uses fillable PDFs and spreadsheets instead.
- Anything built on branded characters (Elf on the Shelf style figures, Disney, Hallmark). Takedown risk.
- Etsy's physical trend items (boots, ruffle tablecloths, brooches). Not digital.

## Global standards (apply to every product)

**Etsy limits and settings**

- Digital files: up to 5 per listing, 20 MB each, file names up to 70 letters, numbers, periods, underscores or hyphens ([Etsy help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628347-How-to-Add-Files-to-a-Digital-Listing)). Some sources say Etsy does not allow variations on digital listings. Check the editor; if unavailable, use separate listings.
- Title up to 140 characters, 13 tags of up to 20 characters, up to 10 photos and 1 video (Etsy's standard limits; confirm in the editor). Every title and tag set in this plan was length-checked by script.
- Etsy's own "up to" figures from [Holiday HQ](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1514470405837): 10+ images lift conversion about 30%, a video lifts sales per impression about 12%, and descriptions of 250+ words lift conversion about 5%. Etsy also asks for clear, accurate titles without keyword cramming.

**Titles and tags**

- Title: the shopper's phrase first (what it is, for whom), then format and use. Plain words, no all caps, no emoji.
- Tags: 13 multi-word phrases, none repeated, mixing product type, occasion, recipient and format. No trademarks (Elf on the Shelf, Disney, Hallmark) and no brand names in titles or tags.

**Creativity Standards and AI** ([policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity), last updated June 10, 2025)

- A digital download of your own design is "Designed by a seller". AI output from your own prompts is allowed, but the description must say AI was used.
- Not allowed: selling AI prompts, or someone else's work (PLR, resold templates).
- Personalised items: the first image must show a finished example, never "Your Text Here".
- Enforcement has been inconsistent ([EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/etsy-calls-itself-human-centered-its-sellers-have-a-more-complicated-story-to-tell/)), so keep a dated provenance folder per product: prompts, model and licence, seeds, source files.
- Disclosure line, used whenever any art is AI-generated: "Design note: original design by \[SHOP\]. The artwork was created with AI image generation from my own prompts, then edited, composed and laid out by me." Spreadsheet-only products can omit it; if unsure, include it.

**Files and licensing**

- Art: prefer code-drawn vector (SVG shapes, patterns, typography) for consistency and speed. Use an image model only for hero illustrations, and only one whose licence allows commercial use of its outputs. No living-artist styles, no Indigenous art styles, no trademarked characters.
- Fonts: SIL OFL or other commercial-use fonts only (Playfair Display, Fraunces, DM Serif Display, Nunito, Caveat), embedded in PDFs.
- Print files: US Letter and A4, 300 DPI at print size, sRGB, 12 mm margins, cut and fold guides where needed. Fillable PDFs use form text fields and ship with a blank version for handwriting.
- Spreadsheets: XLSX that opens in Excel 2016+ and imports into Google Sheets. Formulas only, no macros. Unlocked input cells, locked formula cells (no password), a filled sample copy, and a LibreOffice recalculation with zero errors.
- Delivery pattern within the 5-file cap: `00_START_HERE.pdf` (contents, how to open or print, download help, licence, support note), then the main files. Use ZIPs for sets.
- Licence line: "Personal use: print as many copies as you need for your own household, party, class or office. No resale, sharing or redistribution of the files."

**Description template** (expand each slot; at least 250 words)

```markdown
HOOK: 1-2 sentences, who it is for and the problem it solves, in shopper words.

WHAT YOU GET
- each file: format, pages or tabs, sizes (Instant download. Nothing is shipped.)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Buy and download  2. Print or open  3. Use it

PERFECT FOR
- 3-5 use cases or recipients

DETAILS
- Sizes, file types, works with, colours and style

HOW TO DOWNLOAD
Etsy > You > Purchases and reviews > Download files. Use a browser if the app will not download.

PRINTING TIPS / OPENING IN EXCEL OR GOOGLE SHEETS: one or two lines

DESIGN NOTE: the disclosure line when AI art is used

LICENSE: the licence line

NOTES: colours vary by screen and printer. Digital item, so no returns, but message me if anything is wrong and I will fix it.
```

**Listing images** (10 slots, 3000 x 2250 px, sRGB; every product's spec names its hero and any special slots)

1. Hero: the finished product on-theme, 3-5 big words, readable at 200 px wide, key content centred for thumbnail crops.
2. What's included: labelled grid of every file or page set.
3. Close-up of real filled-in sample content.
4. In use: flat-lay or desk scene.
5. How it works: three numbered steps.
6. Sizes and formats: Letter, A4, file types, "instant download".
7. Palette or style variants, or more sample pages.
8. Print or open guide.
9. Who it is for: four use cases.
10. Trust panel: shop name, personal-use licence, support, bundle upsell.

Video: a slow page flip or tab scroll-through, no audio needed. Mockup scenes: empty backgrounds from an image model or licensed mockups, with real page renders composited by Pillow. Never show anything you do not deliver; label props "not included".

**Pricing and offers**

- Listed prices are starting anchors inside the bands seen (1.50 to 9.63). My rule of thumb: after 14 days and 100+ views, above 3% conversion raise price by $1; below 1%, fix the thumbnail, then the title, before touching price.
- Bundles (separate listings, files zipped): Holiday Organizer (products 1+2+9) $19.90 vs $24.70; Party Host (2+3+9) $22.90 vs $26.70. Buyers of product 6 get product 5's tags as a free bonus file.
- Cyber Specials (Nov 23 to Dec 1): opt in by Oct 27 at 25% off or more to be featured. Opt in bundles and Tier B/C products at listing level and keep the cheapest (2, 5) out. Use Etsy's Sales and Discounts tool only, never an inflated "was" price. Check whether listings published after opt-in are included.

**Build and QA**

- Tools: code execution only. HTML/CSS to PDF (Playwright or WeasyPrint), openpyxl plus LibreOffice recalculation for XLSX, pypdf or reportlab for fillable fields, SVG by code, Pillow for mockups.
- Run a `check_listing.py` before publishing: title up to 140 characters, 13 unique tags up to 20 characters, up to 5 files of up to 20 MB, valid file names, description of 250+ words, AI line present when required.
- Render every PDF page to PNG and inspect it for overflow, margins and Letter/A4 fit. Check each hero image at 200 px wide.

## Product 2: Secret Santa & White Elephant Organizer

A pairing spreadsheet that respects couples and households, plus the printables to run the exchange.

**Buyer and problem:** The organiser of a 6-40 person exchange (office, family, friends) who must avoid pairing partners, collect gift hints and explain the rules.

**Why it should sell:** Gift-exchange printables are everywhere and cheap. A $1.50 questionnaire is listed and many rules sheets are free, so this sells a working tool, not a PDF. It also fits the Friendsmas and friend-gift signals.

**Price and offer:** $6.90. In the Holiday Organizer and Party Host bundles. Not in Cyber Specials.

**Files**

1. `00_START_HERE.pdf`
2. `Pairing_Tool.xlsx`
3. `Printables_Letter.pdf`
4. `Printables_A4.pdf`
5. `Share_Cards_PNG.zip` (invitation and wish-list card for group chats)

**Build spec**

- Names tab, up to 40 people: name, household ID (same ID cannot be paired), a RAND key. Ring method: rank the keys and each person gives to the next person in the ring. That gives one loop, no self-assignments and, for 3+ people, no two-person swaps.
- A validity column checks household clashes. A banner reads "All valid" or the clash count. Reshuffle with F9 (Excel) or by editing any cell (Sheets), then Copy > Paste values to freeze the result. Use only RAND, RANK, INDEX and MATCH, no dynamic arrays.
- Slips tab: one print-ready slip per person, 8 per page, showing giver, receiver, budget, date and place.
- Printables: invitation; four questionnaires (Work-safe, Friends, Family, Kids); wish-list card (sizes, likes, allergies); budget cards with a "Budget: \_\_\_" line; white elephant numbers 1-40; five rule variants (classic, steal limit of two, dice game, left-right pass, no-steal); steal tracker; reveal and thank-you cards.
- Style: modern graphic, berry, cream and evergreen; simple SVG icons (present, mitten, star); Fraunces + Nunito.

**Etsy listing**

- **Title** (137 characters): Secret Santa Generator & Gift Exchange Kit | Excel Pairing Sheet, Printable Questionnaire, White Elephant Rules | Office, Family, Friends
- **Tags (13):** secret santa, secret santa kit, secret santa draw, gift exchange, white elephant, yankee swap, office secret santa, christmas game, gift exchange game, secret santa form, christmas party, friendsmas, name draw
- **Category (confirm in editor):** nearest party-games or paper-templates leaf under Paper & Party Supplies. Attributes: occasion and holiday = Christmas.
- **Description slots:** Hook: draw names in a minute without pairing partners, then print everything. Includes: the five files. Perfect for: office teams, families, friend groups, classrooms. Details: Excel 2016+ and Google Sheets, up to 40 people, Letter and A4. Add the privacy note that the organiser sees all pairs.
- **Hero:** split image with the spreadsheet banner reading "All valid" beside a printed slip and questionnaire, headline "Secret Santa Generator + Printables". Image 4 shows the four questionnaire versions side by side.

**QA gates and risks**

- Simulate 1,000 shuffles for each group size from 3 to 40 with random household layouts. Assert no self-assignments, everyone gives and receives once, one loop. Record how often a shuffle is valid at 20 people with 5 couples and state the typical reshuffle count in the guide.
- LibreOffice recalculation with zero errors. Test Paste values freezing.
- Low price point, so lean on the bundles. "Secret Santa", "White Elephant" and "Yankee Swap" are generic terms.
