## PART B

### [Q1-categories] Best-fit retailer categories / browse-node paths

**Amazon KDP (post-2023 dashboard categories are Amazon's own tree, not raw BISAC — see BISAC note below):**

- Puzzle books (Sudoku, Crossword, Word Search, Word Scramble, Logic Puzzles, Number Puzzles, Cryptograms), all ages: `Books > [Children's Books >] Activities, Crafts & Games > Puzzles` and sibling nodes for Crosswords / Word Games / Board Games / Logic & Brain Teasers, mirrored in the Kindle "Juvenile Nonfiction > Games & Activities" tree for kids/tween titles. Exact node **wording and IDs are `[unverified]`** — Amazon no longer lets sellers request specific browse nodes by email, and the full node tree isn't publicly published; third-party category-browser tools estimate paths like `Juvenile Nonfiction > Games & Activities > Puzzles`, `> Word Games`, `> Board Games`, but these percentages/paths come from scraping tools, not an official Amazon source.
- Coloring books (Animal, Mandala, Nature, Fantasy, Color by Number), adult: `Books > Arts & Photography > Art & Craft Activities > Coloring Books for Adults` (a specific, well-documented node per industry sources); kids equivalent sits under the Children's Books activity tree, exact leaf **`[unverified]`**.
- Guided journals / planners / trackers / mindfulness workbooks: `Books > Blank Books, Journals & Diaries` (general/lined/guided sub-nodes) for journal-style products; dated planners can sometimes qualify for the separate `Calendars` category instead of Books — confirmed as a real distinction, exact sub-node names **`[unverified]`**.
- Handwriting Practice / Activity Book (kids): sits in the Children's Education / Activities crossover; likely dual-homed under `Children's Books > Education & Reference` and `> Activities, Crafts & Games` — split **`[unverified]`**.

**Etsy:**

- Digital printables generally list as either a "finished digital product" or a "craft supply," seller's choice — this is a real, documented Etsy distinction, not a fixed rule per product type.
- Confirmed real taxonomy branch for paper-craft-adjacent digital/physical goods: `Craft Supplies & Tools > Paper, Party & Kids > Papercraft > [Bookbinding/Stationery/etc.]`.
- Planners, trackers, and journal inserts commonly surface under a `Paper & Party Supplies` branch (seen in Etsy's own search-category URLs, e.g. `/search/paper-and-party-supplies?q=...`), but the **exact canonical leaf-node path and ID are `[unverified]`** — Etsy does not publish its full seller-facing taxonomy tree publicly.
- Coloring pages/books on Etsy largely surface through Etsy's own topic/market landing pages (`/market/coloring_pages`, `/market/digital_coloring_book`, etc.) rather than a single fixed category path, and are cross-listed across kids, adult, and craft-supply framings depending on seller choice.

### [Q5-crosslist] Cross-listing fit and category rules to watch

- **Nature Coloring ↔ Animal Coloring**: heavy overlap; most "nature coloring" titles could reasonably cross-list under Animal Coloring and vice versa depending on content mix.
- **Mandala Coloring ↔ Mindfulness Workbook ↔ Gratitude Journal**: adult self-care buyers often search across all three; bundling (coloring pages + prompts) is common and can justify dual categorization on KDP (up to 3 categories/format) and multi-tag use on Etsy.
- **Daily Planner ↔ Habit Tracker ↔ Bullet Journal**: functionally overlapping formats; many top Etsy sellers bundle these as one system rather than separate listings.
- **Logic Puzzles ↔ Number Puzzles ↔ Sudoku**: often shelved together under "Logic & Brain Teasers"-adjacent BISAC/browse nodes; cross-listing is natural.
- **Category rules to watch**:
  - Amazon KDP has an active **low-content/no-content policy** — titles that are essentially blank or near-identical to mass-produced templates can be flagged or de-ranked; puzzle books need genuinely unique puzzle content (not just relabeled generators) to stay compliant and competitive per industry guidance.
  - Amazon eliminated the old practice of emailing support for extra/custom browse-node placement in 2023 — category placement now happens only via the 3 dashboard slots per format plus automatic BISAC/keyword-triggered placement; this is a real, confirmed process change worth designing metadata around.
  - Etsy requires sellers to choose whether a digital item is a "finished product" vs. a "craft supply" — this affects which category/filter buyers find it under, and is a judgment call the seller makes per listing rather than a fixed rule per product type.
  - Quote-based puzzle formats (cryptograms, some journals) must use paraphrased, original, or public-domain quotes — verbatim copyrighted song lyrics, film dialogue, or book passages are not safe to reproduce as puzzle content.
  - Cover/interior art must avoid licensed IP (Disney, Marvel, sports leagues, etc.) even in fan-adjacent niches like "fantasy coloring" or "kids activity book" themes.
